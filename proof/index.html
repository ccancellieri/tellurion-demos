Deliberate boundary

What this evidence does not claim.

  • Public engine source launch pending. Public visitors are directed to the demo source, gallery and verified release until that changes.
  • Italy deployment unavailable. The national release snapshot is inspectable, but its endpoints remain withheld until deployment and post-deployment verification.
  • No production assurance. A working demo does not replace security, capacity, licensing or operational review.
  • No hosted timing claim. Shared-service response times are not benchmark evidence.
  • No public 0.4 distribution. The current public evaluation release is 0.3.0.
